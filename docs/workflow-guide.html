Car Rental Management System

System overview, role-based procedures, and customization guide

This guide describes the current local Kcart Rentals application and the recommended end-to-end workflow for administrators and staff.

Local deployment: PHP 8.1+, MySQL/MariaDB, and Apache on XAMPP. Business timezone is Asia/Manila; displayed money uses Philippine peso formatting.
Updated 18 October 2026 · Customer age, team password, access, reporting, and workflow guidance

01 · AT A GLANCE

How the system works

One shared workspace tracks people, vehicles, reservations, check-outs, returns, invoices, payments, and operating performance.

Customer→Vehicle & availability→Reservation→Reservation payment→Pickup→Return & final billing

Operations

Customer and fleet records support rental booking. Vehicle availability is updated as a reservation or rental changes state.

Billing

Reservation, rental-balance, damage, and late-return invoices share a payment history. Partial payments retain the remaining balance.

Monitoring

Dashboard and Reports show current operating metrics and period-navigable six-month charts. Reports use line, pie, and column visualizations for collections, fleet availability, and rental outcomes.

Local context

Sample customers use Bataan addresses. The app runs on Asia/Manila time and presents amounts in PHP.

Reservation and return rules

  • Rental duration is charged in 24-hour days, with a one-day minimum and partial days rounded up.
  • A configurable reservation fee must be paid before pickup. Pickup can be confirmed at the scheduled pickup time through one hour afterward.
  • Customers must be at least 20 years old. Date of birth is required and checked again by the server when the customer record is saved.
  • After the grace period, uncollected reservations are dismissed and their vehicle is released. No automatic refund is issued.
  • Overdue active rentals are marked Late. Return records mileage and condition, releases the car, and can create damage and configurable per-day late-fee invoices.

03 · ADMIN PLAYBOOK

Administrator: setup and daily operations

1. Sign in. Open the local Kcart URL and use your Admin account. Confirm the Admin role is shown in the sidebar.
2. Secure access. Open Team & access, create a named account per team member, select Staff or Admin carefully, and issue a unique password of at least 8 characters. Deactivate accounts that no longer need access.
3. Configure billing. Open Settings. Set the reservation fee and late-return fee per day to approved business values. Confirm the one-hour pickup grace rule with staff.
4. Prepare records. Add customers aged 20 or older with a verified date of birth and contact details. Add vehicles with plate, specifications, mileage, daily rate, and current fleet status. Upload optional photos/IDs only with customer consent and appropriate handling.
5. Create or oversee reservations. Select an active customer and available vehicle, choose pickup/return date and time, review the estimate, and save. The system reserves the car and creates the reservation-fee invoice.
6. Confirm payment and pickup. Verify the reservation invoice is paid before confirming pickup. The customer must arrive within the scheduled pickup time plus the one-hour grace period.
7. Process vehicle return. Record incoming mileage, vehicle condition, notes, and any approved damage charge. The system completes the rental, updates mileage, releases the vehicle, and creates eligible charge invoices.
8. Reconcile and review. Check Billing for outstanding balances and payment history; use Dashboard and Reports for collection trends, fleet availability, rental states, customer rankings, and CSV exports. Reports offer line, pie, and column charts with previous/next six-month navigation.
9. Sign out. Use the visible Log out button in the account area after each shared-device session.

04 · STAFF PLAYBOOK

Staff: reservation through return

1. Sign in with your Staff account. Confirm the Staff role is shown. Team & access, Settings, and vehicle add/edit/status controls are restricted to Admin.
2. Find or register the renter. Search Customers first. If the record is missing, enter a date of birth confirming the customer is at least 20 and provide accurate contact details. Upload images only when needed and authorized.
3. Check the fleet. Open the read-only Vehicle fleet, search by vehicle type or other details, and use Previous/Next to browse cards (12 vehicles per page). Confirm the selected vehicle is Available and check its rate and details before promising it.
4. Make the reservation. From Dashboard or Rentals, choose the active customer, available vehicle, pickup and expected-return time. Review the calculated day-based estimate and create the reservation.
5. Collect the reservation fee. Open the generated invoice in Billing, select the actual payment method, enter any reference, record the amount, and verify the invoice status/balance. Never record a payment that was not actually received.
6. Handover the car. Open the rental and confirm pickup only after the reservation invoice is Paid and arrival is inside the pickup window. The rental becomes Active and the vehicle becomes Rented.
7. Check in the car. At return, open Returns, choose the open rental, enter the odometer reading, inspect and select the condition, add factual notes, and enter only approved damage charges. Complete the return.
8. Close out billing. Review the rental’s invoices. Collect final balance, damage, or late-fee amounts as applicable; partial payments are supported. Give the customer the printed invoice/receipt where needed.
9. Review and sign out. Use Reports for line, pie, and column chart summaries; navigate across six-month periods as needed and download permitted CSVs. Click Log out before leaving the workstation.

06 · IMAGE AND BRANDING FILES

Where to replace the logo or images

Application logo

Replace the current logo at assets/images/logo.svg. The sidebar, sign-in page, and printed invoices reference this exact path. Keep the filename and SVG format, or update each src reference in includes/header.php, modules/login.php, and modules/billing.php if you use another filename/format.

Customer and vehicle images

These are uploaded in Customers and Vehicle fleet forms; accepted formats are JPG, PNG, and WebP, maximum 5 MB per image. The files are validated then stored as image blobs in the MySQL tbl_customer and tbl_car records—not as loose files in a project image folder. The app serves them through authenticated image routes.

Branding recommendation: Replace assets/images/logo.svg with your approved business logo. If using raster artwork, use a transparent PNG and update the header image path. Keep a backup of the original asset before replacing it.

The project image asset folder is assets/images/. The replaceable logo file is assets/images/logo.svg. There are no bundled customer or vehicle photos; sample records use neutral placeholders until you upload approved images.

07 · LOCAL TEST DATA & RECOMMENDATIONS

Testing, security, and operational recommendations

  • The local database contains synthetic Bataan customer, vehicle, rental, return, invoice, and payment records to exercise dashboards and workflows. These are fictional test records.
  • Local demo logins are admin and staff; their passwords match their usernames. Change both immediately before using real records, sharing access, or exposing the server beyond localhost.
  • New team passwords and password changes must be at least 8 characters. Customer date of birth is required, and customers younger than 20 cannot be saved.
  • Staff can view and search the fleet but cannot add/edit vehicles or change vehicle status; those controls are Admin-only.
  • Use unique named accounts and strong passwords, schedule database backups, and test restoration. Restrict phpMyAdmin and MySQL access to trusted administrators.
  • Before production, define written rules for deposits/refunds, damage assessment, payment reconciliation, document retention, privacy/consent, and late-fee disputes. The current system does not automatically refund a dismissed reservation.
  • Run maintenance.php through Windows Task Scheduler every five minutes if no-show and late statuses must update when the site is idle.
Test workflow checklist: sign in by role → add customer/vehicle → reserve → collect reservation fee → confirm pickup → process return → reconcile invoices → inspect dashboard/reports → sign out.
